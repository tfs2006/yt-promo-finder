YouTube Unlisted Video Finder

Use this guide to understand when hidden YouTube videos can be discovered, how playlist scanning works, and what results to expect from legitimate public-data analysis.

How discovery happens

A YouTube unlisted video finder discovers only unlisted items exposed through public playlists. It does not access private or members-only content.

When results are empty

No results can mean the channel has no public playlists, or unlisted videos were never placed in them.

How It Works

  1. Paste a channel URL, an @handle, or a channel ID, then sign in with Google.
  2. The tool resolves the channel and lists its public playlists, up to 200 playlists per scan.
  3. It checks every video in each playlist for unlisted status, up to 500 unlisted videos per scan.
  4. Matches are deduped and sorted newest first.
  5. Results show each video title, publish date, thumbnail, and the playlist it was found in, with a link to watch it.

Each fresh channel scan uses 5 credits. If the same channel was scanned recently, the saved result is returned and no credits are charged. New accounts start with 3 free credits, plus 1 free credit every day.

What You Will See

A scan returns a summary of how many public playlists were checked, and a list of the unlisted videos found, newest first. Each entry shows the video title, publish date, thumbnail, and the name of the playlist it appeared in, with a link to watch the video on YouTube.

An empty result means the channel has no unlisted videos in its public playlists. It does not mean the channel has no unlisted videos at all. Videos that were never added to a public playlist cannot be discovered this way, and private videos and members-only content are never returned.

Checklist Before You Search

  • Confirm the channel is public and active.
  • Use canonical channel links or exact @handles.
  • Try multiple channels in your niche to benchmark behavior.
  • Re-check over time since playlist composition changes.

YouTube Unlisted Video Finder FAQ

What does a YouTube unlisted video finder scan?

It scans public playlists on a channel and checks the visibility state of included videos. Unlisted videos in public playlists are surfaced.

Why are some channels returning no results?

Many channels keep unlisted videos out of public playlists. If no unlisted items are listed publicly, there is nothing discoverable to return.

How much does a scan cost?

Each fresh channel scan uses 5 credits. If the same channel was scanned recently, the saved result is returned and no credits are charged. Sign in with Google for 3 free credits on signup, plus 1 free credit every day. Credit packs start at $9 for 25 credits.

Can it find private videos?

No. It only finds unlisted videos exposed through public playlists. Private videos and members-only content are never returned.
