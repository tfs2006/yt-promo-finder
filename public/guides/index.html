How it works

The Public Playlist Method Explained

YouTube gives video creators three visibility levels for every video they upload: public, unlisted, and private. Public videos appear in search and on the channel page. Private videos are invisible to everyone except the owner and explicit invitees. Unlisted videos sit in the middle — they don't appear in search or on the channel's public video tab, but anyone who has the direct watch URL can play them.

The key insight the PromoFinder scanner uses is this: creators can add any of their videos — including unlisted ones — to a public playlist. When they do, the YouTube Data API exposes that video's metadata (including its visibility status and video ID) to any authorized API caller reading that playlist. That's the opening the scanner uses. It reads every public playlist on the channel, inspects each video entry's privacy status, and returns any entry whose status is "unlisted".

This method is entirely within YouTube's Terms of Service. Public playlists are designed to be publicly accessible. Reading them through the official API is the intended use of that API. No credentials, no scraping, and no guessing of IDs is involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the PromoFinder unlisted scanner accept as input?

The scanner accepts a YouTube channel URL, an @handle, or a bare channel ID. It does not accept video links, keywords, or playlist URLs as starting points.

How does the unlisted video finder discover unlisted videos?

It checks available public playlist entries within scan limits using the YouTube Data API. Any video that returns an unlisted status is included in results with its watch link.

Is there a free scan available?

Yes. Each browser and network combination gets one free research preview shared across all PromoFinder research tools. Additional fresh scans use credits available on the credits page.

Can the tool find all unlisted videos on a channel?

No. It only finds unlisted videos that the creator has added to a public playlist. Unlisted videos in private playlists or shared only via direct link are not discoverable through this method.
