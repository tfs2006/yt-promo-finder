Expired Domain Finder FAQ

What does the Expired Domain Finder check?

Every match is checked against RDAP to confirm it is available to register, and against the Wayback Machine to confirm it once hosted a real site. Results show the first capture date, the last capture date, and how many times the site was archived.

Does it show traffic or backlink numbers?

No. Visitor counts and backlink counts come from paid data providers and are not measured here. What the tool shows is site history: proof the domain once hosted a real site, how long it ran, and how often it was archived. That is the honest signal, and it is all that is claimed.

Why did my keyword return no results?

The search covers the most recently deleted domains, and only names that pass the availability check and have site history are shown. A narrow keyword may simply have no match in the current batch. Try a broader keyword, another ending, or check back after the list refreshes.

How much does a search cost?

10 credits per search. The search is charged when it runs, including when no domains match your keyword. Nothing is charged when the search itself fails.
