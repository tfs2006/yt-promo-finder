How the Sponsorship Estimate Is Calculated

The estimate starts with the channel's average views per video, measured across up to 100 videos from the past 12 months. That average is divided by 1,000, multiplied by the niche's average CPM, and multiplied by 10, because sponsors typically pay about 10 times the ad CPM for an integration. The result is the midpoint estimate. The low estimate is half the midpoint and the high estimate is 1.5 times the midpoint.

Niche CPM ranges used by the tool. Finance runs $20 to $50 per 1,000 views (average $35), tech $15 to $30 (average $22), beauty, fitness, and travel $10 to $28 (averages $17 to $20), gaming and education $8 to $20 (average $14), lifestyle $5 to $18 (average $12), food $6 to $16 (average $11), and entertainment $5 to $15 (average $10). Channels that match no niche use a default range of $8 to $20 (average $14).

Creator tiers. The channel is also placed in a size tier by average views per video: Micro Influencer at 1K to 10K, Small Creator at 10K to 100K, Medium Creator at 100K to 500K, Large Creator at 500K to 1M, and Mega Influencer at 1M and above. Engagement rate, meaning likes plus comments divided by views, is shown alongside the estimate for context.

Actual deal terms can vary by format, deliverables, exclusivity, audience demographics, and negotiation. Use the range to plan and compare, not as a price to quote.

YouTube Sponsorship Rate Calculator FAQ

How much do YouTubers charge for sponsorships?

YouTube sponsorship rates depend heavily on niche, audience size, engagement, and deal structure. Smaller creators may charge hundreds per integration, while larger creators can charge thousands or more.

What is a good CPM for YouTube influencer marketing?

Sponsorship CPM can be materially higher than standard ad CPM and changes by niche, audience quality, deliverables, and rights. Use this estimator as a planning benchmark, not a fixed quote.

How is a sponsorship rate calculated for YouTube?

Rates are calculated by multiplying a creator's average views per video by an estimated CPM for their niche, then adjusting for engagement rate, audience quality, and placement type. A dedicated sponsor video commands a premium over an integrated mid-roll mention. This tool estimates that range using live channel data.

How does the tool tell sponsored videos apart from regular videos?

A video is treated as sponsored when its description links to a website outside the usual social media domains. The tool then compares the average views of those sponsored videos with the channel's non-sponsored videos. It is a heuristic based on public descriptions, not a confirmed deal record, so some sponsored videos may be missed and some linked videos may not be paid placements.
